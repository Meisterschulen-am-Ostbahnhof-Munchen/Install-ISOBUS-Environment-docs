# DIN Rail Moped Connectors

This photo series documents the correct wiring and connector types used for the "DIN Rail Moped" module.

## 1. Basic Wiring and Connections

The module's basic wiring on the DIN rail is done via red and black wires (power supply and ground), which are connected to the circuit board's pin headers using spade connectors.

* **Connector Type:** 6.3 mm spade connectors are used as standard.

## 2. Insulation and Touch Protection

To prevent short circuits between the closely spaced contacts and to ensure touch safety, the spade connectors are insulated. Red and black insulating sleeves (or heat shrink tubing) completely enclose the crimp points and the rear part of the connectors.

<img src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/69573151/131330811-2f7aac87-ee6a-43b9-9b88-493c8a4dabf7.jpg" />

## 3. Detail View: Special Signal Connections

For specific connections or in confined spaces, different sizes are used. The image shows the use of **two 2.8 mm spade receptacles** mounted side-by-side on a terminal block. This is important for contacts that are not mechanically designed for the wider 6.3 mm receptacles.

<img src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/69573151/131330813-dfbfb0aa-dbbf-4bfe-945a-b828850712d3.jpg" />
2x 2.8 mm spade receptacles

## 4. Connector Housing and Overall View
